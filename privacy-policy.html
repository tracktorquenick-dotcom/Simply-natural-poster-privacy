Data deletion instructions : ``` Privacy Policy – Simply Natural Poster Effective date: 26 October 2026 Simply Natural Poster is a personal app owned and used only by Nick Winthrope. It posts content to and manages his own Facebook Page "Fun With Nature" and his own YouTube channel. What data the app uses - Facebook Page ID - Facebook Page access token - YouTube channel information (channel ID and name) - OAuth access tokens for Facebook and YouTube How the data is used This data is used only to post and manage content on Nick's own Facebook Page and YouTube channel. Nothing else. Who uses the app Nick Winthrope is the only user. The app does not collect data from any other person, visitor or follower. Sharing No data is sold, rented or shared with any third party. Data goes only to Facebook (Meta) and YouTube (Google), and only as needed to post content. Storage Tokens and IDs are stored only on Nick's own device inside the app. They are not stored on any external server. Data deletion instructions To delete all data used by the app: 1. Facebook: go to Settings & Privacy > Settings > Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), find "Simply Natural Poster" and click Remove. 2. YouTube/Google: go to myaccount.google.com > Security > Third-party connections, find "Simply Natural Poster" and remove access. 3. On the device: disconnect the accounts in the app or uninstall it. This deletes all stored tokens and IDs. You can also email the address below to ask for deletion. Requests are handled within 30 days. Contact amara.nick@mail.grokbot.com ```
